Robot BOM builder

Photos → evidence-linked parts → human review → verified bill of materials

1. Upload robot photos

Top view plus underside and close-ups of board labels give the best result. Parts that are not visible are never assumed.

2. Visible components

Seen in at least one photo. Confirm or reject each line, correct counts, and enter make / model / SKU only if you verified them on the part.

3. Unverified / likely hidden

Not seen in any photo. These are possibilities and open questions, not confirmed parts.

4. Issues and compatibility checks

Rule-based critical issues close only when the component data is corrected (e.g. specs: voltage_v=7.4; current_a=1.2).
